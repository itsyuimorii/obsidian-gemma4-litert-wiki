Recording deck

Twenty-two scenes, one page each. Go fullscreen, press Space to step through — every take comes out identical, because nothing here is random. The window is a mock of Obsidian in its light theme; the plugin ships no colours of its own, so what it looks like is entirely the host theme's decision.

The order is the loop, not the UI. Notes go in, questions come out, answers go back in, and then five scenes on the part that keeps that from rotting. Scenes 01 → 22 read as one continuous story in one vault; any single act also stands alone.

Reading rather than stepping? Everything it does, most important first — the same features written out, one plate each.

Recording

  • SpaceNext step. → and Enter do the same; clicking anywhere also advances, which is easier on a trackpad.
  • RStart over. Every take is identical — no randomness anywhere in the scripts.
  • HHide the chrome — hint bar, scene nav, and the narration strip. Press this before you hit record.
  • TToggle the scene title card.
  • PToggle the PROTOTYPE badge. Leave it on for anything public.
These are scripted mock-ups built for recording, not the running plugin. Layout, copy, command names, folder paths and modal titles are taken from the source; the numbers are representative and no model runs on this page. Nothing here is a live benchmark.
