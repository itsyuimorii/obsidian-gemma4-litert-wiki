Grind size and extraction

Grind size sets how much coffee surface the water can reach. Finer grounds expose more area, so compounds dissolve faster.

Too fine, and the shot over-extracts into bitterness; too coarse and it under-extracts, thin and sour.

Most guides put the target extraction window at 18–22% for espresso, adjusting grind before anything else.
