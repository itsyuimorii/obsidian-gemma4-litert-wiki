Composer concepts — Claudian-inspired

Claudian's signature: one bordered composer card containing everything — context pills, a borderless textarea, and a toolbar inside the same box (tools left, send right). Three ways to apply that to Gemma Wiki. Suggestion chips are text-only in all three.

A · Unified composer (full Claudian)

One card: pills → tall borderless textarea → hairline-separated toolbar with [+] [⚡] [⤢] left and send right. Chips stay above as outlined pills. Most similar to Claudian/Claude.ai; tallest.
