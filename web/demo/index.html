Improve — the only feature that writes to a raw notenext
A long Chinese note is split on headings into passes that each fit a 4096-token context, rewritten one at a time, and stitched back. Structure and typos only; the wording is yours.
 Improve 09
Concept page — one tag, gathered across everythingnext
Pick a tag two or more pages share; the plugin writes a page above them that links down. Flat notes acquire a second storey.
 Concept page 10
Tags — a vocabulary, and a retag that respects deletionsnext
schema.md is a note, so the rules are yours to edit. Retag proposes; a tag you deleted by hand goes on the rejected list and does not come back.

Provenance — the key points get checked against the notenext
Each extracted point goes back to the raw note to find the sentence it came from. The one that cannot be traced is flagged rather than quietly kept.

Recording

  • SpaceNext step. → and Enter do the same; clicking anywhere also advances, which is easier on a trackpad.
  • RStart over. Every take is identical — no randomness anywhere in the scripts.
  • HHide the chrome — hint bar, scene nav, and the narration strip. Press this before you hit record.
  • TToggle the scene title card.
  • PToggle the PROTOTYPE badge. Leave it on for anything public.
These are scripted mock-ups built for recording, not the running plugin. Layout, copy, command names, folder paths and modal titles are taken from the source; the numbers are representative and no model runs on this page. Nothing here is a live benchmark.
